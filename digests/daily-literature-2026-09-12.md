### Adds context [PubMed]: Olivine and dissolved alkalinity trigger different bacterial community shifts in water and oyster gills: insights from a mesocosm experiment
Antoni et al., 2025 · PMID: 41852431 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41852431/

This mesocosm study chronically exposed European flat oysters (*Ostrea edulis*) to alkalinity-enhanced seawater from two OAE methods (olivine weathering vs. dissolved NaOH) and tracked shifts in water and gill bacterial communities. High-alkalinity olivine treatments favored potentially pathogenic *Vibrio* taxa in the gill microbiome, while dissolved-alkalinity treatments produced comparatively minor shifts. This offers a plausible biological mechanism for the lab's delayed mortality pattern: if OAE exposure similarly shifts the oyster microbiome toward opportunistic pathogens, a lag before infection-driven mortality manifests would be consistent with a late-onset (rather than acute) mortality curve.
