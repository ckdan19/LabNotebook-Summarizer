### Adds context [PubMed]: The ammonia transporter gene family and its immune regulatory role in the Pacific oyster (Crassostrea gigas)
Sun et al., 2026 · PMID: 41861978 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41861978/

This study characterized the ammonia transporter (AMT) gene family in Crassostrea gigas and found that one member, CgAMT1, is expressed predominantly in haemocytes and upregulated under immune challenge. Under sustained pathogen (Vibrio splendidus) exposure, ammonia progressively accumulated in haemocytes alongside rising CgAMT1 expression, and this accumulation was linked to haemocyte death, outlining an ammonia-associated immune cell death pathway. This offers a mechanistic lens for the parallel water-chemistry-trial tank's elevated ammonia and nitrite: even though only 2 mortalities have been recorded so far across the trial, this paper suggests ammonia stress can erode immune cell viability progressively, so gross mortality counts alone may understate physiological strain building up in the high-ammonia tank.
