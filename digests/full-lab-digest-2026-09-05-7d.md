- **Date**: 2026-08-31
- **URL**: https://robertslab.github.io/sams-notebook/posts/2026/2026-08-31-Resazurin-Assays---USDA-M.gigas-Juveniles-OAE-Preconditioning-Effect-on-36C-Heat-Stress-Response/
- **Author**: Sam White
- **Categories**: resazurin, Magallana gigas, Crassostrea gigas, Pacific oyster, heat stress, OAE
- **Key finding**: Sam ran a resazurin metabolic assay (fluorescence read every 30 min for 3 hr in a Synergy HTX plate reader) on 46 OAE-preconditioned and 46 control juvenile *Magallana gigas* (Pacific oyster) that had been exposed to 36°C heat stress, following 6 hr of OAE preconditioning at 10°C performed by Steven.
