### Adds context [PubMed]: Olivine and dissolved alkalinity trigger different bacterial community shifts in water and oyster gills: insights from a mesocosm experiment
Antoni et al., 2025 · PMID: 41852431 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41852431/

This mesocosm study chronically exposed European flat oysters (Ostrea edulis) to Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement via two methods — olivine weathering and dissolved NaOH — at two concentrations, then compared bacterial community shifts in the water and in the oyster gill microbiome against untreated controls. Olivine-based alkalinization caused much larger shifts in water bacterial communities (including a rise in potentially pathogenic Vibrios at high alkalinity) than dissolved NaOH, while the gill microbiome response scaled more with alkalinity concentration than with alkalinization method. This does not test heat stress or resazurin/mortality outcomes directly, but it shows that OAE method and dose materially change host-associated physiological/microbial responses in oysters, which is relevant context for why the notebook's OAE-exposed juveniles and adults might show variable, inconsistent stress-assay signals — the exposure method and concentration used in each trial could plausibly be shifting host physiology in ways that decouple resazurin signal from simple mortality.
