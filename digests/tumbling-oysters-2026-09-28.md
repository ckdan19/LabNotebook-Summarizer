### Oil vs. Control Ranks 9 of 10 Label Splits
- **Date**: 09-26-2026
- **Author**: Steven Roberts
- **Categories**: Epigenetics, Genomics
- **Key finding**: Reanalysis of public MBD-BS data (SRP139854) from oil-exposed vs. control eastern oyster (*Crassostrea virginica*) gill asked whether oil exposure changes DNA methylation. After substantial QC work (fixing adapter-dimer trimming, filtering incompletely bisulfite-converted reads, handling PBAT-style strand bias, and dropping a low-yield sample), permutation testing across all 10 possible ways to label 3 controls vs. 2 oil-exposed samples showed the real split ranks only 9th of 10 for both differentially methylated CpGs and regions — differences track individual samples (notably the most/least globally methylated ones) rather than treatment. Conclusion: no detectable oil effect at this sample size, though a general gene-body/exon methylation landscape for the species is characterized as a byproduct.

### Olympia Oyster lcWGS on the New Chromosome Assembly
- **Date**: 09-28-2026
- **Author**: Steven Roberts
- **Categories**: Genomics, Computing
- **Key finding**: Restored and re-aligned 112 Olympia oyster (*Ostrea lurida*) low-coverage WGS samples to the newly released chromosome-scale xbOstLuri2 assembly (replacing the fragmented Olurida_v081 reference), then reran ANGSD-based genotype-likelihood PCA, admixture, and Fst analyses. Results confirm three Puget Sound genetic groups (South/Central Sound, Hood Canal, north Olympic Peninsula) and resolve two mislabeled collections: "WB" is genetically Coos Bay stock (Fst = 0.037 vs. Coos Bay, not a San Juan Island wild population) and "MB" groups with Hood Canal rather than its assumed South Sound identity — both flagged for correction against collection records. A Tajima's D split by sample-naming convention is attributed to library batch effects rather than biology.
