### Adds context [PubMed]: Towards sustainable oyster farming: A field-based modeling approach in Hong Kong
Sheik Oli et al., 2026 · PMID: 41338171 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41338171/

This field-based modeling study used a full-factorial central composite design across salinity, chlorophyll, dissolved oxygen, and nitrite to predict growth and survival of Hong Kong oysters (*Crassostrea hongkongensis*) across real aquaculture sites.
