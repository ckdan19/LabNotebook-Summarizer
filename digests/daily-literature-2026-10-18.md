## Tank room, survival assays, and water chemistry trial

**Source:** Jesse Lowe, 2026-10-17 · https://genefish.wordpress.com/2026/10/17/tank-room-survival-assays-and-water-chemistry-trial-4/

### [Adds context] [PubMed]: Towards sustainable oyster farming: A field-based modeling approach in Hong Kong
Sheik Oli et al., 2026 · PMID: 41338171 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41338171/

Modeled growth and survival of the oyster *Crassostrea hongkongensis* across three aquaculture environments using a central composite design that included salinity, chlorophyll, dissolved oxygen, and nitrite as covariates, finding that salinity and chlorophyll were the dominant drivers of survival while nitrite played a comparatively minor role, with survival reaching 100% under optimized conditions. This supports interpreting the lab's observation that oysters survived the 48-hour hardening check despite one tank's elevated nitrite and reduced alkalinity: nitrite in this concentration range may be a secondary stressor relative to other water-chemistry parameters.
